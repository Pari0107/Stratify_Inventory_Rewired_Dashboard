Inventory Rewired

Simulation and optimization of inventory decisions for Retail Craft Pvt. Ltd.

Project Overview

Our objective was to simulate and optimize inventory and replenishment decisions across Retail Craft's supply chain network, with the goal of reducing out-of-stock events and excess inventory holding costs by 10% over a 3-month simulated horizon.

Business Challenge

Retail Craft faced critical inventory imbalances: high out-of-stock rates on fast-moving products led to lost sales, while overstocking of slow-movers inflated holding costs.

Data Preparation

The initial 12-week dataset contained significant inconsistencies that required thorough cleaning before analysis.

Data Issues Identified

  • Sales recorded when inventory was zero
  • Missing entries in the transaction logs
  • Inconsistent date formats and store identifiers
  • Purchase orders not properly allocated across stores

Cleaning Process

  • Corrected sales entries with zero inventory
  • Added missing entries based on transaction patterns
  • Standardized date formats and store codes
  • Implemented Inverse_ratio.py algorithm for PO allocation

Data Split Strategy

We used 9 weeks of data (March 1 to May 7) for model training and reserved the last 3 weeks for testing and validation against our model's predictions.

Replenishment Model: S-s Policy

We implemented an S-s inventory policy that automatically triggers replenishment when stock falls to the reorder point (s) and orders up to the target level (S).

Key Components

  • Reorder Point (s): Inventory level triggering a replenishment order
  • Order-Up-To Level (S): Target inventory level after replenishment
  • Safety Stock: Buffer for demand and lead time variability
  • Lead Time: Duration between order placement and receipt

Formulas Used

s = Forecast Demand during Lead Time + Safety Stock

Safety Stock = Z × σd × √L

Order Quantity Q = S - Current Inventory

Model Simulation

We simulated inventory levels across all three stores over a 3-week horizon, comparing our model's recommendations against actual historical data.

Simulation Approach

  • Tracked daily inventory levels, purchase orders, and stockouts
  • Used Inverse_ratio.py algorithm for store allocation
  • Balanced inventory distribution to minimize localized shortages
  • Respected shelf life constraints for perishable items

Outputs Generated

  • Stockout reports by SKU and store
  • Purchase order logs with delivery dates
  • Daily inventory snapshots
  • Performance metrics comparison
